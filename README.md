*Per aspera ad astra — through hardship, to the stars.*

Everyone else starts with a fleet. You start with one world, and a very long way to go.

Ad Astra lets you begin Stellaris as a civilization confined to its homeworld. Choose one of ten historical starting ages, climb through history by research, build a space program from nothing, and emerge into a galaxy that did not wait for you.

## 1.4 "Galileo" — what's new

1.4 is the biggest update so far. It is available as a public beta; the scope is frozen and only test-driven fixes remain before release.

**Research drives the ascent.** Every period technology advances the *Beyond the Stars* situation through a historical prerequisite tree: no passive timer, no scripted waves. Each technology states what it unlocks, and building slots, urban capacity and district specializations open with the discoveries that make them plausible.

**A true pre-FTL civilization.** The engine now treats your empire as a genuine pre-FTL civilization: an era crest from day one, awareness, observation posts and era hardships. Awareness is told from the ground, space fauna and foreign visitors are perceived through your age's eyes, and **the empires you detect stay nameless** until someone introduces themselves.

**A space program paced by your telescopes.**

1. **Astronomy** — observatory, radio telescope, space telescope. Each observation campaign reveals one neighbouring system without surveying it.
2. **First steps** — suborbital launch, first satellite, crewed flight, lunar landing if you have a moon, and a repeatable deep probe.
3. **Exploration** — a sublight science ship surveys your home system on your orders.
4. **Construction** — a construction ship, then a starbase you build yourself.
5. **Orbital infrastructure** — a starport with modules and a building, a defense platform, and your first economic station.
6. **Hyperspace** — the last programme, then Hyperdrive.

**Living under an occupied sky.** If another empire takes your home system, the *Reclaim Our Sky* decision lets its ethics decide: pacifists and xenophiles cede it, authoritarians demand tribute, traders sell it, and xenophobes or militarists refuse — which gives you a claim to take it back by war.

**An economy that arrives with history.** An electrical grid grace period, no modern blockers at early starts, colony designations locked to your era until emergence, and a free government reform when you emerge.

**Art and texts.** Custom icons for period technologies, telescopes and decisions, new event artwork, and a full English and French text pass.

The complete list is in the [1.4.0-beta.8 release notes](https://github.com/ampynjord/ad-astra-stellaris/releases/tag/beta-1.4.0-beta.8).

### Known issues in the beta

- A first-contact chain can still reveal a star empire before emergence.
- The homeworld can vanish from the outliner at the Bronze Age; it stays reachable from the galaxy map.
- A cosmic storm or the Energy Mogul trait can involve energy before electricity exists.
- The Great Khan notification can appear before emergence.

## What you play

### Day one

Choose an age from Stone to Early Space. Starting earlier is harder, but improves your final **Legacy of the Ascent**. You also choose an ascent pace, and whether the rest of the galaxy develops normally or waits for your emergence.

| The ground | The world | The sky |
|---|---|---|
| Stone · Bronze · Iron | Late Medieval · Renaissance · Steam · Industrial | Machine · Atomic · Early Space |

### The ascent

The **Beyond the Stars** situation tracks your journey through the ages. Researching the period technologies of your age moves it forward, and prerequisites keep discoveries in historical order. Choose an approach for the current age: careful continuity for stability, a forced march for speed at a social cost, or eyes on the sky for research at the expense of unity.

### An economy that arrives with history

Resources appear only once your civilization can use them: alloys with bronze, consumer goods with steam, energy with electricity. Period capitals and buildings take your world from a ring of stones to modern ministries, and national programmes let you steer your society for a few years at a time.

### The road to space

In the Space Age, base-game founding technologies are researched rather than granted, and appear only when the gameplay they support exists. Telescopes chart the neighbourhood, milestones take you to orbit, and programmes provide a sublight science ship, a construction ship and, finally, the path to Hyperdrive.

### Emergence

Research **Hyperdrive** to complete the ascent. Your ships are refitted for FTL travel, the normal technology tree opens, and your **Legacy of the Ascent** becomes permanent. In 1.4 it combines a bonus that grows with how far back you started and one specialization of your choice: scientific, industrial, civic, pioneer or orbital.

## Compatibility

| Item | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Stellaris | 4.4.x "Pegasus" |
| DLC | None required |
| Languages in game | English and French |
| New game | Required after updates |
| Achievements | Disabled, as with any mod |

The mod overrides starting and early-tier technologies, selected civilian ship sizes, scripted triggers such as `is_regular_empire`, several casus belli and war goals, and focus cards. Those are the most likely conflict points.

Known reports: **Gigastructural Engineering & More** can leave the starting colony without a city district, and **Ethics and Civics Classic** can interfere with the capital chain. Please include your complete load order when reporting a compatibility problem.

## Roadmap

The roadmap is a direction, not a release calendar: a version ships only after its targeted in-game tests pass.

- **Now — 1.4 "Galileo".** Public beta; the remaining work is test-driven fixes and the release gates: Atomic, Renaissance and Stone starts, the full chain up to Hyperdrive, save migration, and civic, ethic, trait and DLC combinations.
- **Next — 1.5.** Scope to be defined once 1.4 is stable.
- **Long term.** Nomadic arks, generation ships, Gestalts and deeper planetary politics may come to Origins after prototypes and playtesting — never as separate mods or promises.

**Ad Astra: Origins** is the only Ad Astra mod and depends on no other mod. Whatever it may need one day will be built in-house.
