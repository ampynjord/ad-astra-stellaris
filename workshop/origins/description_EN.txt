[h1]Ad Astra — Origins[/h1]
[i]Per aspera ad astra — through hardship, to the stars.[/i]

[quote]Everyone else begins with a fleet. You begin with a world — and the first question is whether it can look beyond its own horizon.[/quote]

Ad Astra turns Stellaris into a historical ascent. Begin as a [b]pre-FTL civilization[/b], choose how far back your story starts, and build the knowledge, economy and orbital infrastructure needed to join a galaxy that has never stopped moving.

[b]No DLC required.[/b] English and French included.

[b]How to play:[/b] create or edit an empire and choose the [b]Ad Astra[/b] origin. Without it, the game starts normally. Start a new game after updating.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]1.4 "Galileo"[/h2]

The biggest update so far.

[list]
[*][b]Research drives the ascent.[/b] Every period technology advances the Ascent situation through a historical prerequisite tree, and states exactly what it unlocks.
[*]Your empire is a [b]true pre-FTL civilization[/b] in the engine's eyes: era crest, awareness, observation posts, and first contact seen from the ground.
[*][b]Awareness told from the ground:[/b] four chapters follow your awareness level, and sightings, signals and abductions only happen when someone is actually watching.
[*]Star empires you detect [b]stay unknown[/b] until someone introduces themselves.
[*]The Early Space Age is paced by your [b]telescopes[/b]: astronomy charts new systems without surveying them.
[*][b]Living under an occupied sky:[/b] if another empire takes your home system, reclaim it through a negotiation decided by its ethics — or by war.
[*]A [b]Legacy of the Ascent[/b] that grows with how far back you started, plus one specialization of your choice at emergence, and a free government reform.
[*]Original icons and event artwork, and a full English and French text pass.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h2]The ascent[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Ten starting ages[/b], from Stone to Early Space. The earlier the beginning, the harder the climb — and the stronger the final Legacy.
[*][b]250 original period technologies[/b] across physics, society and engineering. Each age is a real research tree with meaningful prerequisites.
[*][b]An economy that arrives with history.[/b] Bronze introduces alloys, steam introduces consumer goods, electricity introduces energy. Capitals, buildings and specializations unlock through the discoveries that make them possible.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h2]The Early Space Age[/h2]

There is no gifted fleet and no magical leap from a launchpad to an empire.

[list]
[*][b]Astronomy:[/b] observatory, radio telescope, space telescope.
[*][b]First steps:[/b] suborbital launch, first satellite, crewed flight, a lunar landing if your system has a moon, and a deep probe.
[*]Launch a [b]sublight science ship[/b] and survey your home system on your own orders.
[*]Build a [b]construction ship[/b], then turn your first outpost into a starport with modules and stations.
[*]Complete the [b]Hyperspace Programme[/b] and research Hyperdrive to emerge as a spacefaring empire.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Compatibility and support[/h2]

[b]Stellaris v4.5.x[/b] (tested on 4.5.4) · No DLC required · Achievements disabled · Start a new game after a major update.

The mod overrides early technologies, selected civilian ships, scripted empire triggers, several casus belli and war goals, and focus cards. Those are the most likely conflict points.

[b]Known compatibility reports:[/b] Gigastructural Engineering & More can leave the starting colony without a city district; Ethics and Civics Classic can interfere with the capital chain. Please include your complete load order, starting age, game date and [i]error.log[/i] when reporting a problem.

[b]Known issue:[/b] while another empire holds your home system, your homeworld leaves the outliner. It stays yours and reachable from the galaxy map.
