[h1]Ad Astra — Origins[/h1]
[i]Per aspera ad astra — through hardship, to the stars.[/i]

Everyone else starts with a fleet. You start with a planet, and a very long way to go.

Begin Stellaris as a [b]pre-FTL civilization[/b] confined to its homeworld. Choose a historical starting age, build a society through ten ages, create a space program from nothing, and emerge into a galaxy that did not wait for you.

[b]No DLC required.[/b] The mod is playable in English and French.

[h2]What you do[/h2]

[b]Day one.[/b] Choose one of [b]ten starting ages[/b], from Stone to Early Space. Earlier is harder and earns a stronger final [b]Legacy of the Ascent[/b]. Choose your ascent pace and whether the galaxy develops normally or waits for your emergence.

[b]The ascent.[/b] Each age contains [b]twenty-five period technologies[/b] across all three research fields. They arrive in five waves, in the order of their historical dates. The ascent waits at the end of an age until every remaining technology has been discovered.

[b]The economy.[/b] A resource appears only when your civilization can use it: alloys with bronze, consumer goods with steam, and energy with electricity. Period capitals and buildings take the world from a ring of stones to modern ministries. Exploitation campaigns offer meaningful short-term trade-offs.

[b]The road to space.[/b] In the Space Age, base-game [b]founding technologies[/b] must be researched instead of being handed to you. Space programs then provide sublight exploration, a starbase yard, an optional orbital fleet, and the route to Hyperdrive. Your science ship [b]surveys by presence[/b], mapping one body per month even in a claimed home system.

[b]Emergence.[/b] Research Hyperdrive to finish the ascent. Your fleet is refitted for FTL travel, the normal technology tree opens, and the Legacy becomes permanent. If another empire occupies your home system, emergence is negotiated.

[h2]Roadmap[/h2]

[b]1.4 — The Sky Fills[/b] adds observatories, radio telescopes, satellites, crewed flight, deep probes, first landings, launch risks, and a clearer propulsion ladder.

[b]1.5 — The World Below[/b] adds a planet that grows by region, rival nations and unification, period traditions, and industrial choices that shape the Legacy.

[b]Later[/b]: staged terraforming and frontier outposts, then observation, contact, and a playable archaeology of each run's own history. This is direction, not a release calendar.

[h2]Support and compatibility[/h2]

Stellaris v4.4.x · No DLC required · New game required after updates · Achievements disabled.

The mod overrides early technologies, selected civilian ships, scripted empire triggers, several casus belli and war goals, and focus cards. Those are the most likely conflict points.

[b]Known compatibility problems under investigation:[/b] Gigastructural Engineering & More can leave the starting colony without a city district; Ethics and Civics Classic can interfere with the capital chain. Please share a full load order when reporting a conflict.
