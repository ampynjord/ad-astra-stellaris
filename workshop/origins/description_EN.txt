[h1]Ad Astra — Origins[/h1]
[i]Per aspera ad astra — through hardship, to the stars.[/i]

[quote]Everyone else begins with a fleet. You begin with a world — and the first question is whether it can look beyond its own horizon.[/quote]

Ad Astra turns Stellaris into a historical ascent. Begin as a [b]pre-FTL civilization[/b], choose how far back your story starts, and build the knowledge, economy and orbital infrastructure needed to join a galaxy that has never stopped moving.

[b]No DLC required.[/b] English and French included.

[b]How to play:[/b] create or edit an empire and choose the [b]Ad Astra[/b] origin. Without it, the game starts normally.

[b]1.4 public beta available[/b] on [url=https://github.com/ampynjord/ad-astra-stellaris/releases/tag/beta-1.4.0-beta.8]GitHub[/url] — details below. This Workshop page keeps the stable 1.3.2.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]The ascent[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Ten starting ages[/b], from Stone to Early Space. The earlier the beginning, the harder the climb — and the stronger the final [b]Legacy of the Ascent[/b].
[*][b]250 original period technologies[/b] across physics, society and engineering. Each age is a real research tree: five ranks, meaningful prerequisites, and no technologies injected into your queue.
[*][b]Research is progress.[/b] Every completed period technology advances the Ascent situation. Reach an age's final discoveries to open the next chapter.
[*][b]An economy that arrives with history.[/b] Bronze introduces alloys, steam introduces consumer goods, electricity introduces energy. Capitals, buildings and specializations unlock through the discoveries that make them possible.
[*][b]A route to the stars you build yourself.[/b] Astronomy, first launches, satellites, crewed flight, probes, sublight exploration, construction ships, an outpost and orbital infrastructure come before Hyperdrive.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h2]The Early Space Age[/h2]

There is no gifted fleet and no magical leap from a launchpad to an empire.

[list]
[*]Develop the foundations of science, industry and propulsion.
[*]Launch a [b]sublight science ship[/b] and survey the home system from within it.
[*]Build a [b]construction ship[/b], then create your own first [b]outpost[/b].
[*]Add modules and orbital stations before preparing the Hyperspace Program.
[*]Research [b]Hyperdrive[/b] to emerge as a normal spacefaring empire.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility and support[/h2]

[b]Stellaris v4.4.x[/b] · No DLC required · Achievements disabled · Start a new game after a major update.

The mod overrides early technologies, selected civilian ships, scripted empire triggers, several casus belli and war goals, and focus cards. Those are the most likely conflict points.

[b]Known compatibility reports:[/b] Gigastructural Engineering & More can leave the starting colony without a city district; Ethics and Civics Classic can interfere with the capital chain. Please include your complete load order, starting age, game date and [i]error.log[/i] when reporting a problem.
